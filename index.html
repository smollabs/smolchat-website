Small AI models, running on your own device

smolchat is an open-source app for your computer and your phone. Download a small language model once, then chat privately and offline. It is not a browser chatbot, and nothing leaves your device.

Pick a small model that fits

Download the one you want, then chat offline. Smaller sizes start faster on modest hardware; larger ones are generally more capable.

Sizes are parameter counts: M = million, B = billion.
