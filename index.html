Desktop app for Windows and macOS

Small AI models, running on your own computer

smolchat is an open-source desktop app by SmolWorks. Download a small language model once, then chat privately and offline. It is not a browser chatbot, and nothing leaves your machine.

Chat without the cloud

smolchat brings small language models to a native desktop window. Pick a model, download it, and start talking. Your conversations are stored locally, and the model runs on your hardware.

Two separate downloads. You download the smolchat app from this site. Model weights are downloaded later, from inside the app, from the model repositories it is configured to use. This website never runs or hosts models.

Questions

Does smolchat work offline?

Yes, once the app and at least one model are downloaded, conversations run locally without an internet connection.

How do I get models?

From the Model Library inside the app. Weights are downloaded once from the configured model repositories, such as Hugging Face, and kept on your computer.

Is my data private?

Inference happens on your computer and chat history is stored locally. The website itself has no backend and does not receive your conversations.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows and macOS desktop installers are offered on this page.

What hardware do I need?

Hardware requirements have not been verified yet, so none are listed. They will appear here once confirmed.

Do I need an account?

The site and installers do not ask for sign-in. Confirm account details in the project documentation.
